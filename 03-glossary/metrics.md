# PAY 4.2 glossary — metrics

What a datapoint measures. Every datapoint carries exactly one of these.

> **Definitions.** The labels below are transcribed verbatim from the annotated table
> layout. They are the framework's own wording, not a legal definition. Where a precise
> definition is needed, cite EBA Guidelines on fraud reporting under PSD2 (EBA/GL/2018/05, as amended) rather than paraphrasing this file.


| Code | Metric | Unit | Templates |
|---|---|---|---|
| `qAFY` | Amount of losses | €£$ | Y_01.02, Y_02.02, Y_03.02, Y_04.02, Y_05.02, Y_06.02 |
| `qAJN` | Amount of payment | €£$ | Y_01.01, Y_02.01, Y_03.01, Y_04.01, Y_05.01, Y_06.01, Y_07.01, Y_08.01 |
| `qGAE` | Number of transactions | # | Y_01.01, Y_02.01, Y_03.01, Y_04.01, Y_05.01, Y_06.01, Y_07.01, Y_08.01 |

`€£$` marks a monetary amount reported in the reporting currency; `#` marks a count.
All datapoints in this framework are constrained to non-negative values.
