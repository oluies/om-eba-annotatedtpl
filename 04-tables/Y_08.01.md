## What this template reports

`Y_08.01` reports **transactions initiated by payment initiation services providers**, measured as Amount of payment and Number of transactions, split across domestic, cross-border within the EEA, and cross-border outside the EEA.
Each reported figure is one datapoint identified by a stable numeric id; the id is the
only identifier that is unique on its own, because row and column codes repeat across
templates and variants.
